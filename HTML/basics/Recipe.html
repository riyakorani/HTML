🍕 Pizza Recipe

Ingredients

  • Pizza Base
  • Cheese
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Onion
  • Capsicum

Steps

  1. Spread sauce on pizza base.
  2. Add vegetables and cheese.
  3. Bake for 15 minutes.
  4. Serve hot.
